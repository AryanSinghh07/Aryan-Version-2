## Overview

A computer-vision system for the Rashtriya Gokul Mission that identifies and scores elite indigenous bovine breeding stock.

## Problem

Manual animal type classification depends on human observers, which brings bias and fatigue into decisions about breeding stock.

## Solution

A CNN-based scoring model built with TensorFlow and OpenCV, served through FastAPI, that classifies animals consistently. Built for Smart India Hackathon 2025.
