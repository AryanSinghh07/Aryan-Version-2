## Overview

A 24/7 AI-powered Cognitive Behavioural Therapy platform for students, with a real-time chatbot built on LangChain and Ollama.

## Problem

Students often need support at odd hours, such as 2 AM, when no one else is available to talk to.

## Solution

A chatbot that streams responses in real time and guides students through grounding techniques: 5-4-3-2-1, guided breathing, meditation and mind-training games. It was selected as a finalist at the Galgotias × IEEE Code Astraa Hackathon 2025.
