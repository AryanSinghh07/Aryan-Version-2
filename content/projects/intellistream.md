## Overview

A stealth-gamified cognitive profiling platform that predicts academic stream fit for Indian Class 10–11 students.

## Problem

Stream selection usually rests on a single high-stakes aptitude test.

## Solution

Students play games while the platform analyses their behaviour, replacing the one-off test with continuous, naturalistic measurement. An XGBoost model served through Flask predicts stream fit, with a React and TypeScript front end on Supabase.
