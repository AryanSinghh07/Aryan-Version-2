## Overview

A field-image crop disease detection system. A farmer photographs a leaf, and a computer-vision model identifies the disease so it can be treated early.

## Problem

Crop diseases are often spotted too late, and expert diagnosis is not always available in the field.

## Solution

A TensorFlow CNN pipeline with OpenCV preprocessing classifies diseases from field images, served through a React web app backed by MySQL. The system was presented live to 2,000+ stakeholders at UP International Trade Show 2024, where it was praised by agriculture and industry representatives.
